# Ines and Daniel

**Why not:**

He is on the island for only a year, and she has promised herself never to wait for anyone again.

**What binds them:**

He takes her ferry at six every morning, and there is no other.

**The steps:**

Silence, weather, a medical run in the night, a dinner, a fight, a kiss, a yes.

**The heat level:**

Half-open door, with their first night in chapter 19.

**The midpoint and the break-up:**

He takes a permanent job on the mainland without telling her, and she hears it from the passengers.

**The dark night and the gesture:**

He turns down the mainland before he asks her, so she never has to wait for an answer.

**The ending:**

One winter evening the ferry is stuck in the ice, and he waits on the dock without checking the time.
