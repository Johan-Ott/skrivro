# Checklist for the first draft

Check these before you start polishing the prose.

**The couple**

- Do they meet early, within the first tenth of the book?
- Does each have a goal, a life and a wound of their own?
- Does each have a lie about love that the relationship challenges?
- Is there an outside reason that keeps forcing them together?
- Does the tension build in steps, and does the chemistry show in the scenes?
- Is it clear in every intimate scene that both want it?

**The plot**

- Is the midpoint roughly halfway, and does it feel then as if this could work?
- Is the break-up rooted in their wounds, not in a misunderstanding?
- Do both do something active to find their way back, not just one of them?
- Does the grand gesture match the wound it heals, and is the other free to say no to it?
- Does the reconciliation get time, and does whoever did the hurting truly apologise?
- Is the ending happy, and does the reader get to see it in a scene of its own?

**The world around them**

- Do the side characters have lives of their own, and do they push or help the relationship?
- Does the place matter, and does the reader want to be there?
- In feel-good: is the threat to the place tied to what happens between the couple?
- In feel-good: does the lead's new life have an arc of its own, even without the love story?

**The tone**

- Does the book keep the tone and heat level the first chapter promised?
- In romantic comedy: is there at least one laugh per chapter, and does it come from the characters?
- Do most chapters end on something that makes the reader go on?
