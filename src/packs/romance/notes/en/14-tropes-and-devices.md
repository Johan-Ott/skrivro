# Tropes and devices

How a trope works is in the guide Tropes and promises. Here are the most common ones. Make one note per trope with the template The trope in Relationship.

- **Enemies to lovers.** _Works when_ they are evenly matched and the hatred has a real cause. _Cliché when_ the hatred is a misunderstanding that vanishes at the first kiss.
- **Fake relationship.** _Works when_ the act demands closeness and the rule costs something when it cracks. _Cliché when_ nobody notices they stopped pretending.
- **Only one bed.** Part of forced proximity. _Works when_ the closeness is both obstacle and relief. _Cliché when_ it is a backdrop for a bed scene without feeling.
- **Grumpy/sunshine.** _Works when_ both have a reason and the sunshine is hurt too. _Cliché when_ the grump only needs saving by cheerfulness.
- **Second chance.** _Works when_ the cause was real and both have changed. _Cliché when_ what broke is easily excused.
- **Forced proximity.** _Works when_ the place forces out what they avoid. _Cliché when_ nobody tries to get away.
- **Friends to lovers.** _Works when_ the friendship itself is at stake. _Cliché when_ one has been in love all along without showing it.
- **Marriage of convenience.** _Works when_ the deal is clear and turns into a cage. _Cliché when_ nobody can say why they don't simply divorce.
- **Forbidden love.** _Works when_ the ban has a price and both know it. _Cliché when_ it is a father who is merely cross.
- **Coming home.** _Works when_ the place and the past press on them. _Cliché when_ the city is always emptiness and the small town always the answer.
- **Slow burn.** _Works when_ every scene moves them a little. _Cliché when_ they talk past each other for three hundred pages.
- **Third act breakup.** _Works when_ it grows out of the wounds they have carried all book. _Cliché when_ a misunderstanding that one conversation would solve pulls them apart.
- **Brother's best friend.** _Works when_ loyalty to the brother is a real choice and the obstacle can be read from both partners' wounds. _Cliché when_ the brother is only a guard who lets them through in the last chapter.
- **Secret identity.** _Works when_ the lie gives them a closeness they would not otherwise have dared, and the reveal costs. _Cliché when_ the one who lied is forgiven without it showing.
- **Rivals.** _Works when_ the competition mirrors what both lack and a win threatens the relationship. _Cliché when_ it is easy to see who wins.

**Make it yours.** Change one condition, not the trope: who holds the power, what the rule costs, who cracks first.
