# The relationship arc (template)

Rename the note to the couple's names.

**The trope:** which trope, or tropes, carry the relationship? What does it promise the reader, and what turn of your own do you give it?

**Why not:** each person's reasons this can never become anything. One outer and one inner reason each.

**What binds them:** what forces them to keep meeting? A job, an inheritance, a bet, a fake relationship.

**The chemistry:** what sparks? How do they argue, how do they laugh, what do they notice about each other?

**The steps:** five to seven steps from the first meeting to love. For example: irritation, a favour, an honest talk, an almost-kiss, the kiss, a night, an "I'm staying".

**The heat level:** closed door, half open or open? Where in the book do the intimate scenes fall?

**The midpoint:** what happens, and why does it look as if it will hold?

**The break-up:** what drives them apart, and how is it rooted in both their wounds?

**The dark night:** what does each of them understand when they are alone?

**The grand gesture:** who does what, and why does it heal exactly the wound the book showed?

**The ending:** happily ever after or happy for now? Which image shows it will hold?
