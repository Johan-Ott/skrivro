# The ally (template)

Rename the note to the character's name. The ally helps the main character, and is often the one who betrays them or falls when things are darkest.

**What they bring:** a skill, a contact, a place to hide, or a voice that tells the truth.

**Why they help:** friendship, debt, money, self-interest? The more ambiguous, the more tension.

**The contrast:** how are they different from the main character? Careful where the main character is reckless, or certain where the main character doubts. An ally who thinks differently brings conflict to every conversation.

**What they cost:** what do they take from the main character, even while helping? Time, safety, a trail that leads the villain right to them, a secret that leaks.

**The secret:** what do they know or have they done that the main character does not know about?

**Where loyalty is tested:** which chapter, and what is at stake for them?

**Betrayal or sacrifice:** do they betray, die, or choose the main character at a high price? Why then?

**What the reader should believe:** should the reader trust them, distrust them, or change their mind? Which seeds do you plant?

**Afterwards:** what is left of the relationship, and what does it mean for the main character's arc?
