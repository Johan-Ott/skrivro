# The main character (template)

Rename the note to the character's name.

**Ordinary life:** where do they stand when the book opens? Work, home, routines, and what is already chafing.

**What they stand to lose:** a person, a life, a secret, a freedom. Make it concrete and show it in the first chapter.

**Competence:** what are they good at that will be needed? It does not have to be weapons. A nurse knows how people lie about pain, an auditor sees when the numbers do not add up.

**Vulnerability:** what makes them easy to hurt? A body, an addiction, a child, a fear.

**Why them:** why can they not just walk away and let the police handle it? Close that exit early.

**Wound:** what in their past makes this particular threat hit hard?

**The lie they believe:** for example "I can't trust anyone" or "I'm not the kind of person who fights". The threat will force them to test it.

**Want and need:** what they want (survive, protect, expose) and what they must learn in order to succeed.

**The line:** something they would never do. Will they cross it at the end, or win precisely by refusing to?

**Under pressure:** how do they react when it counts? Freeze, turn cold and methodical, do something reckless?

**Voice:** how do their thoughts sound when they are afraid? Write three sentences they think as someone tries the door handle.

**Arc:** who are they in the last chapter, and what has it cost?
