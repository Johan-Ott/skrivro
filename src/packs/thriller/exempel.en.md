# Ylva Brand

**The goal:**

She wants the city to see, just once, what it cost to cut corners on the water.

**Why they think they are right:**

Her son died in a flood that the inquiry called bad luck.

**Resources and method:**

She has the codes to every pumping station, and her guards obey without asking.

**Why the main character:**

The main character wrote the inquiry, and has a daughter who lives downstream.

**The first time:**

She cuts the water pressure across the north of the city for three minutes and restores it before the alarm goes off.

**The mask:**

The calm expert the city calls when something goes wrong.

**The line they cross:**

She locks her own head of security inside a pumping station about to flood.

**The weakness and the end:**

She has to see it happen with her own eyes, so she is there herself when it does.
