# The clue list (template)

Rename the note to the name of the case. Copy the block below once for every clue and every red herring. Fill it in as you write, and read it through before you write the reveal.

**The clue:** what is it? An object, something someone says, a reaction that does not fit.

**Truth or red herring:** does it point towards the culprit, or away? If away, what is the real explanation?

**First appearance:** the chapter and scene where the reader sees it.

**How it is hidden:** in a list, behind a dramatic moment, under another character's wrong reading?

**Second appearance:** where does it return and raise a question?

**What the reader thinks:** what does the reader take it to mean when it appears?

**What it really shows:** its true meaning.

**Fair play:** is it on the page before the detective draws the conclusion? Note the chapter.

**When the detective understands:** the chapter, and what makes it click.

**Resolution:** for a red herring, where does the reader get the real explanation, and is it as concrete as the herring was?

**Tagged in the text:** does the scene carry the label Clue or Red herring?
