# What really happened (template)

The crime as it actually happened, in time order. The reader never sees it like this, but you need it for everything to add up.

- **Before:** what led up to the crime.
- **The crime:** time, place, how.
- **After:** what the culprit did to hide it.
- **The traces left:** every clue, and where it is.
