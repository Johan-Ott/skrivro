# Common pitfalls

- **The solution comes out of nowhere.** A clue the reader never got to see feels like cheating.
- **The culprit is too obvious.** Give more suspects equally strong reasons.
- **The detective gets lucky.** Let the solution come from work, not chance.
- **The reveal becomes a lecture.** Rather show the pieces fall into place in a scene with tension.
- **The middle sags.** A second crime or a turn in the middle keeps the pace up.
