# The promises of crime fiction

A crime reader expects a puzzle that can be solved, and a solution that feels fair.

- **The crime early.** A crime in the first chapter, or at the latest a tenth into the book.
- **The culprit is there.** The reader has met them before the reveal, preferably early.
- **Fair play.** Every clue the solution needs is shown to the reader before the detective understands it.
- **The detective drives.** The solution comes from their work and wit, not from chance or a confession.
- **An answer to why.** The motive should make sense, even when it is terrible.
