# Checklist for the first draft

Check once the draft is done:

- Does the crime come early enough?
- Has the reader met the culprit before the reveal?
- Does every clue the solution rests on show before the detective understands it?
- Does every suspect have a motive, an opportunity and something to hide?
- Does the red herring lead the reader astray without lying?
- Does the timeline add up, with alibis that hold or crack in the right place?
- Does the case cost the detective something?
