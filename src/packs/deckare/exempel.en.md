# Maj-Britt Holm

**The distinct trait:**

Writes down the plate number of every car that parks badly by the harbor.

**The tie to the victim:**

They shared a mooring for twenty years and stopped speaking in May.

**Motive and opportunity:**

The victim had found the hole in the club funds, and she has a key to the clubhouse.

**The alibi:**

Home in front of the TV all evening, but the neighbor saw her car was gone.

**The lie and what they hide:**

Her brother gambles, and she has covered his debts from the club funds.

**When suspicion lifts:**

In chapter 14, when the ER records show she sat there with her brother.

**What they know:**

One of her notes is about the culprit's car, but she thinks it was a tourist.
