# Beta reader questions (template)

Rename the note to the reader's name. One reader per note. Ask what they felt and did, not what you should change. Ask them to make notes as they read, not afterwards.

**The reader:**

<!-- Do they read the genre? That decides how you weigh the answers. -->

**Where they put the book down:**

<!-- Which chapter, and why? A break in the middle of a scene counts most. -->

**Where they got tired or skipped:**

<!-- Pages, chapters or scenes. If several readers stop at the same spot, you have found something. -->

**What they felt:**

<!-- What did they feel, and is that what you wanted there? -->

**What they guessed:**

<!-- What did they think would happen, and when? -->

**What they did not understand:**

<!-- Who was who, what had happened, why someone acted. Rarely the reader's fault. -->

**Who they rooted for:**

<!-- Who did they fail to get a grip on? -->

**What they remember a week later:**

<!-- A scene, a line, an image. That is what carries the book. -->

**The ending:**

<!-- Did the ending feel fair, and give more than they expected? -->
