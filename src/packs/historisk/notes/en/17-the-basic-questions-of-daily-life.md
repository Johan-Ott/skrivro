# The basic questions of daily life

What makes the reader believe in a period is daily life. Not the kings, but what people ate, what it cost, how long something took. Ask the questions below for your period and place, and answer with something that can show in a scene.

**The body.**

- **Food:** what did people eat on an ordinary day, and when? What was festive food, what was hardship?
- **Light and warmth:** how did they light a room, how did they heat it, what did it cost?
- **Hygiene:** how did they wash themselves and their clothes, and how often?
- **Illness and death:** what did people die of, who cared for the sick, what was done with the dead?
- **Sleep:** where and with whom did people sleep?

**Money.**

- **What did an ordinary person earn, and what did bread, beer, a shirt and a pair of shoes cost?** Convert to daily wages and the reader will understand.
- **Did people pay with coins, goods, labor or credit?**
- **Who owned the land, and who was allowed to work it?**

**Time and space.**

- **How far could one travel in a day, and how?**
- **How did people get news, and how old was it?**
- **How was time measured:** clocks, church bells, the sun, seasons, holidays.
- **What could be seen on the horizon, and what was unknown?**

**Power and law.**

- **What was your character allowed to do, own and decide?** Sex, class, age, place and faith decided a great deal.
- **Who judged, and by what rules?** What happened to those who broke them?
- **Who could read and write, and what was written down?**

**Faith.**

- **What was taken for granted about God, illness, dreams and nature?**
- **What was it permitted to doubt, and by whom?**

**How to use them.** Answer the questions in the notes Epoch and Setting. Then read a chapter and see which groups do not show at all. That is where the period wears thin. It need not be clear or explained. The reader feels the difference between a scene where someone eats, and a scene where someone eats the same porridge for the fourth day running.

**Try this:** choose an ordinary day in your protagonist's life and write only what happens from dawn to night, without drama. You will see what you lack knowledge of.
