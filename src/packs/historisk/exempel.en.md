# Västerbotten, Sweden, 1867 to 1869

**Time span:**

The famine years, when the harvest failed year after year and people ground bark into their bread.

**Power and the law:**

The sheriff arrests, the district court judges, and stealing food can mean prison on bread and water.

**The social order:**

The farmer owns and inherits, the maid has a yearly contract, and a married woman is her husband's ward.

**Faith:**

To the pietists, liquor, card games and dancing are sin, and they meet in kitchens to read the Bible themselves.

**Daily life and the year:**

Haymaking, the autumn slaughter and the market in the church village rule the year.

**Money and measures:**

People count in riksdaler, and a day's pay does not buy a barrel of rye.

**Illness and distance:**

A letter to Stockholm takes more than a week, and children die of hunger and the illnesses that follow it.

**What does not exist yet:**

The railroad up here, the krona coin and the word teenager.
