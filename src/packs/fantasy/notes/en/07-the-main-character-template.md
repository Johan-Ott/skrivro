# The main character (template)

Rename the note to the character's name.

**Wants:** what are they chasing when the book begins? Something concrete you can see, not just being happy.

**Needs:** what must they understand or let go of to make it through the ending?

**The lie they believe:** the false belief about themselves or the world that the journey will break.

**Home:** where they come from, and what they take for granted about the world because of it.

**The ability:** magic, skill or heritage. What can they do, and what does it cost them each time?

**Their place in the world:** class, people, faith. Who looks up to them, and who looks down on them?

**What they can't do:** a weakness the world or the enemy will exploit.

**The voice:** how do they sound, in thought and in speech? A phrase or a curse that only they use.

**The other life:** in urban fantasy, the job, the family or the rent that the hidden world threatens. What do they lie about, and to whom?

**What they risk:** the personal stakes, next to the big ones.

**How they change:** who are they in the last chapter, and which choice shows it?
