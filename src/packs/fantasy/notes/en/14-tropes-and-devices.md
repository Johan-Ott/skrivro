# Tropes and devices

A trope is a device the reader recognizes and wants: the chosen one, the mentor, the secret beneath the city. The trope is the promise, the execution is yours.

- **The journey and the quest.** _Works when_ the road changes those who walk it. _Cliché when_ it is a list of places.
- **The dark lord.** _Works when_ the evil has a reason the reader understands. _Cliché when_ it is only dark.
- **The chosen one.** _Works when_ being chosen is a burden and a choice that costs. _Cliché when_ they are best at everything from the start.
- **The mentor.** _Works when_ the mentor has their own reasons and flaws. _Cliché when_ they die just as the student needs them most.
- **The enemy who becomes an ally.** _Works when_ trust is built slowly and can break. _Cliché when_ one gesture is enough.
- **The hidden heir.** _Works when_ the inheritance is a debt to settle. _Cliché when_ they merely get a crown.
- **Magic awakens.** _Works when_ it costs and goes wrong first. _Cliché when_ it works on the first try.
- **The school for the hidden.** _Works when_ the rules and rivalries carry. _Cliché when_ the school is a sign with no work behind it.
- **The ancient object.** _Works when_ it changes the bearer. _Cliché when_ it is a shortcut to power.
- **The forgotten people.** _Works when_ they are people with inner disputes. _Cliché when_ they exist to help the hero.
- **The secret beneath the city.** _Works when_ the logic of why nobody knows holds. _Cliché when_ a secret society only needs to be cool.
- **The prophecy.** _Works when_ it can be read in several ways and people act to fulfill or prevent it. _Cliché when_ it is a fixed truth waiting for its chosen one.
- **The dragon or the great beast.** _Works when_ it is more than a threat: a witness, a will or a sacrifice that changes what the fight is about. _Cliché when_ it only guards a hoard.
- **The war that never ended.** _Works when_ old wounds steer today's politics and no one has clean hands. _Cliché when_ there is always an evil side to point at.

**Make it yours.** Change one condition, not the trope: who is chosen, what it costs, who carries the object, where the hidden lies. If you build the book on one trope, break it once, on purpose.
