# Checklist for the first draft

Go through this once the whole draft is written, before you start polishing sentences.

**The idea and the world**

- Can the book's what if be said in one sentence, and is it felt in every part?
- Does the world shape the plot, or could the story have happened anywhere?
- Is there at least one scene of wonder in each part?

**The magic**

- Has the reader seen the cost of magic paid before magic solves anything important?
- Is the final battle decided by rules established earlier?
- Is there something magic can never do, and does that limit matter to the plot?

**The characters**

- Does the main character want something concrete from the first chapter?
- Do they make the decisive choices themselves, especially at the end?
- Does the antagonist have a goal that makes sense from their own point of view?
- Do the companions have goals of their own, which sometimes clash with the main character's?

**The storytelling**

- Does the first chapter open in action, with only a handful of new names?
- Are there lines where someone explains something both characters already know?
- Do the chapters end on a question, a decision or a discovery?
- Does something happen on every journey, to earn its place in the book?

**The ending**

- Is the main conflict resolved in this book?
- Can we see what victory cost?
- Has the main character changed, and does the ending show it in action?
