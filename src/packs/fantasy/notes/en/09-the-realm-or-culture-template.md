# The realm or culture (template)

Rename the note to the name of the realm or people.

**In one sentence:** what is the first thing a stranger notices here?

**The land:** climate, landscape, what grows and what people eat. How does the land shape its people?

**Power:** who rules, how did they get there, and who wants to take it from them?

**Faith:** what do people believe, and what is forbidden? What do they swear by when they hit their thumb?

**Everyday life:** work, feasts, mourning, how people greet each other. A custom an outsider gets wrong.

**Internal conflicts:** poor against rich, old against young, believers against doubters. What do people argue about over dinner?

**The neighbours:** who do they trade with, who do they fear, and who have they fought?

**Living history:** an event everyone still talks about, and that different groups remember differently.

**The view of magic:** revered, forbidden, ordinary, or a commodity?

**What the main character takes for granted:** what they don't even see, because they grew up with it.
