# The knots

**What the magic does:**

A knot in a rope holds a weather, a fever or a promise until it is untied.

**Who and how:**

Only those taught by a living knotter, and their fingers go numb with cold.

**The rules:**

One knot holds one thing. Whoever unties it gets what was bound.

**The cost:**

Every knot takes one word from the knotter's memory, forever.

**The limits:**

It never binds the dead, never the knotter's own feelings, and never on someone else's rope.

**The world around the magic:**

The harbor towns pay well for wind knots, and the priests call knotters thieves of the gods' weather.

**Where the reader learns it:**

In chapter 2 a fisherman buys a wind knot, in chapter 5 the apprentice forgets her sister's name.
