# Checklist for the first draft

Go through the list when the draft is done, before you start rewriting.

**The threat**

- Do you know what the threat is, what it wants and which rules it follows, even if the reader never gets the full picture?
- Is every rule shown in action at least once before it decides anything?
- Does the threat ever break its own rules? Then it needs an explanation, or the moment needs to go.
- Does the reader see the threat too early or too clearly?

**The escalation**

- Is each part of the book more dangerous, closer or more personal than the last?
- Is there a clear first price, where the reader realises anyone can be next?
- Does the worst scene come late enough?

**The main character**

- Do they have a private fear that the threat presses on?
- Do they have a believable reason to stay, or a clearly closed way out?
- Do they act, or does everything just happen to them? Let them make decisions, bad ones included.
- Has the reader had time to care about them before it gets dangerous?

**The isolation**

- Is help cut off step by step, in a way that feels plausible?
- Has the text answered why nobody calls the police?

**The rhythm**

- Are there quiet spells between the strong scenes?
- Does every chapter end with something that makes the reader want to go on?

**The ending**

- Is the confrontation decided by the rules and the main character's choices, not by luck?
- Does the ending answer what the fear was really about?
- Is there a last chill that follows the reader out of the book?
