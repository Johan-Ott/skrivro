# The place (template)

Rename the note to the name of the place: the house, the farm, the island, the school or the block where it happens.

**The place by daylight:** how it looks when all is normal. What is beautiful or safe about it.

**The place in the dark:** how the same rooms change at night, in fog or when the power goes.

**The rooms that matter:** the three or four spots where the key scenes happen, and what is in each.

**The forbidden spot:** the cellar, the attic, the bricked-up door, the lake. Where the main character does not want to go, and in the end must.

**The sounds:** what creaks, drips and hums. Which sounds belong here, so you know when a sound is wrong.

**The history:** what has happened here before. Who lived here, and what the neighbours say and do not say.

**Why it is hard to leave:** distance, weather, money, a contract, a feeling that the place holds on to you.

**How the place isolates:** signal, roads, nearest neighbour, how long help takes to arrive.

**How the place changes through the book:** what breaks, moves or is discovered, bit by bit.
