# Tropes and devices

The trope is the promise, the execution is yours.

- **The haunted house.** _Works when_ the house has a history that acts in the present and a human fear underneath. _Cliché when_ it only creaks and goes cold.
- **The isolated place.** _Works when_ the isolation is logical and no one can call for help for a reason in the story. _Cliché when_ the phone simply has no signal.
- **The witness no one believes.** _Works when_ the doubt applies to them too. _Cliché when_ every adult is stupid at exactly the right moment.
- **The curse.** _Works when_ rules and origin are planted. _Cliché when_ an old book in the last chapter explains everything.
- **Possession.** _Works when_ the change is small at first and those close notice. _Cliché when_ the eyes simply turn black and the voice deep.
- **The town with a secret.** _Works when_ the threat is the community itself. _Cliché when_ the locals are one evil mass that exists only to threaten the guest.
- **The child who sees.** _Works when_ the child's view is credible and costs the adults. _Cliché when_ it is just a creepy smile and a song.
- **The group that splits up.** _Works when_ the decision comes from real fear or loyalty. _Cliché when_ it comes from stupidity.
- **The monster with rules.** _Works when_ the rules are revealed in steps and cost something. _Cliché when_ the rule happens to be what the hero has just got.
- **The last unease.** _Works when_ it grows from what has happened. _Cliché when_ it is always a hand from the grave.

**Make it yours.** Change one condition, not the trope: the place, who isn't believed, what the rule costs.
