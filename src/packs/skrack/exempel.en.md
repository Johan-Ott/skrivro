# The neighbor

**What it is:**

Something that lives in the empty houses of the new development and copies whoever moved out last.

**What it wants:**

It wants to be invited home by someone who means to stay.

**The limits:**

It cannot leave the development, and it can only copy someone it has seen through a window.

**The omens:**

The lights next door come on in the wrong order, and mail arrives at a house that stands empty.

**When it shows:**

It waves with the wrong hand and wears the same sweater every day.

**The rules:**

Chapter 4: never outside the development. Chapter 9: only someone seen through a window. Chapter 15: only someone who calls it by name, and that decides the ending.

**The unexplained:**

Where it came from, and why it chose this development.

**What it stands for:**

The fear of being replaceable, that no one on the street would notice if you were swapped.
