# Tropes and devices

The device is the promise, the execution is yours.

- **The turning point.** _Works when_ you show both before and after, and what it cost. _Cliché when_ a single moment explains everything.
- **The first memory.** _Works when_ the memory carries the book's question. _Cliché when_ it is a pretty image with no connection.
- **The letter or document.** _Works when_ the text has its own voice and you read it from the distance of the present self. _Cliché when_ you quote to avoid writing.
- **The reckoning with one person.** _Works when_ you understand the other too. _Cliché when_ they are the villain and you the victim.
- **The case study.** _Works when_ the case is concrete and contradicts something the reader believes. _Cliché when_ it is always a success story that confirms the idea.
- **The model with a name.** _Works when_ it orders something the reader already knows. _Cliché when_ you squeeze everything into the model.
- **Addressing the reader as you.** _Works when_ you know where the reader stands. _Cliché when_ you address them like a pupil.
- **The exercise.** _Works when_ it gives an answer the reader did not have before. _Cliché when_ it is a questionnaire in mid-chapter.
- **The recurring image.** _Works when_ its meaning shifts each time. _Cliché when_ it is explained at the end.
- **The opponent who is right.** _Works when_ you actually change your mind a little. _Cliché when_ the objection is a straw man.
- **The place you return to.** _Works when_ the place has changed in a way that shows how you have changed. _Cliché when_ it is a nostalgic trip where everything is forgiven.
- **The silence in the family.** _Works when_ you show what was never said and what it cost. _Cliché when_ a secret in the last chapter explains everything.
- **The object that carries the memory.** _Works when_ the object has a history that changes with what you learn. _Cliché when_ it is only a symbol you point at.

**Make it yours.** Change one condition, not the device: who is telling, what the device costs, where it shows.
