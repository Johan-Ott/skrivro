# The chapter card for non-fiction (template)

Rename the note to the chapter's working title. Fill one in for each chapter of an explanatory or guiding book, before you write it.

**The reader's problem:**

<!-- What is unclear, hard or tempting for the reader when they begin the chapter? -->

**What the reader can do afterwards:**

<!-- Understand, choose or do: one sentence, with a verb. -->

**What the reader first believes:**

<!-- Which belief or habit should the chapter change? -->

**The key example:**

<!-- Which case, person or experiment shows the idea? -->

**The objection:**

<!-- Which objection or exception must you take up, so the reader trusts you? -->

**Evidence:**

<!-- Which sources carry the claims, and how certain are they? -->

**Exercise or next step:**

<!-- Is it needed, and what does it give that the text doesn't? -->

**The bridge to the next chapter:**

<!-- Which question does this chapter leave open? -->
