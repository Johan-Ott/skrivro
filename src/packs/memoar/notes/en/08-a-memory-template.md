# A memory (template)

For memoir, and for personal non-fiction where your own memories become examples. Rename the note to the memory in a few words, like "The last summer at the farm". Make one for each memory you want to use.

**When and where:** year, season, place. As exactly as you can, and how sure you are.

**The first image:** the detail the memory has caught on. A colour, a sound, a smell.

**Who was there:** and what each of them did.

**What happened:** in order, without interpretation. Only what a camera would have seen.

**What was said:** the lines you remember, word for word or roughly.

**What you felt then:** in your body and your thoughts, in the then-self's words.

**What you understand now:** what the memory means today, in one or two sentences.

**The gaps:** what you don't remember. Don't fill it in silently; say you don't remember, or guess openly.

**Scene or summary:** does the memory need to play out in real time, or is one paragraph enough?

**Checking it:** who else remembers it, and do they remember it differently? Are there photos, letters, diaries or newspapers from that day?

**Its place in the book:** which chapter, and how the memory moves the book's question forward.
