# A person in my life (template)

Rename the note to the person's name, or the name they will have in the book. Make one for each person who matters to the story.

**Who they are to you:** parent, partner, friend, boss. And who they were to others, which you may not have seen.

**First memory:** the first picture you have of them. Where, when and what they were doing.

**What's typical of them:** a phrase, a gesture or a habit that brings them alive on the page. "She always folded the paper in four before she read it."

**Their role in the story:** how they bear on the book's question. Do they help, block, betray or rescue?

**Their side of it:** how they would tell what happened. What did they know that you didn't?

**What you owe them:** fairness, gratitude, an apology, or simply being seen as a whole person.

**What's sensitive:** health, secrets, others close to them who could be hurt. What must go in, and what can stay out?

**Name and recognisability:** real name or changed? Which details need changing to protect them?

**Reading it:** will they get to read their passages before the book is done? If so, what did they say?

**Where they appear:** the chapters they show up in.
