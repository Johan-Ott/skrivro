# Examples: memoirs and non-fiction that do it well

Read them as craft: look for how they do it, not what happens. The sentences are our own.

- **I Am Zlatan Ibrahimovic**, David Lagercrantz and Zlatan Ibrahimovic (2011). A strong voice on the page, and a way of letting a life become a story.
- **Educated**, Tara Westover (2018). How a narrator can keep the then-self and the now-self apart, and be honest about what she knew and didn't know.
- **The Year of Magical Thinking**, Joan Didion (2005). How a bounded year and a clear question can carry a whole book, in a restrained language.
- **Sapiens**, Yuval Noah Harari (2011). How a vast span of time is turned into a story with a clear question in each chapter.
- **Mårbacka**, Selma Lagerlöf (1922). A childhood home and a district as living memory, with warmth and distance in the same gaze.
- **Atomic Habits**, James Clear (2018). How a simple model, examples and short chapters make a method easy to remember and use.

**Read like this:**

- Which question is asked at the start, and how does it carry the book?
- Where does the author show and where do they interpret?
- How does the book handle what it doesn't know?
