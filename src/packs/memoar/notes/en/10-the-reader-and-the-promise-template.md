# The reader and the promise (template)

Rename the note to the book's working title. Fill it in before you write the first chapter, and reread it whenever you lose your way.

**The reader:** a real kind of person, not "everyone". Age, situation and what they already know. "Parents of teenagers who have stopped talking to them."

**The problem:** what the reader is struggling with, in their own words. What have they already tried?

**Why now:** what makes them pick up the book right now.

**The promise:** in one sentence, what the reader can do, know or change after the last page.

**What the book doesn't promise:** the boundary. What the book deliberately leaves to others.

**Your right to write it:** experience, research, profession or a journey of your own. Why should the reader trust you?

**The core idea:** the one thing the reader should remember if they forget the rest.

**The chapter claims:** one claim per chapter, in order. Does each build on the one before?

**The examples:** the cases, people or experiments that carry the book. Which is strongest, and where should it go?

**The tone:** teacher, colleague, friend or guide? Do you speak to the reader as "you"?
