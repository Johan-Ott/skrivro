# Grandma in the stern

**When and where:**

Summer 1994, maybe 1995, on my grandmother's lake in Dalsland.

**The first image:**

Her hands on the oars, with her wedding ring on the wrong hand.

**What happened:**

She let me row us home by myself and sat still in the stern without a word for an hour.

**Then and now:**

Then I was proud, and now I think she already knew she was ill.

**The gaps:**

I do not remember how we got ashore or who met us.

**The check:**

My cousin stood on the dock, and there is a photo with a date on the back.

**Scene or summary:**

A scene, because the silence in the boat is the middle of the chapter.
