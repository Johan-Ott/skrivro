# Nora

**Age and narrator:**

Fourteen, and she tells it herself, in first person.

**Wants and needs:**

She wants to win the local paper's writing contest, but needs to dare show someone what she really writes.

**The problem:**

Her former best friend has read her notebook and threatens to post it.

**Why the adults cannot fix it:**

She promised not to tell why they fell out, and her mom would call the friend's parents at once.

**Strength and weakness:**

She hears everything said between the lines, but always answers with a joke.

**The voice:**

Angry she goes short, happy she talks too fast, and embarrassed she starts talking about the weather.

**Home:**

Mom and her little brother in a small apartment, and Dad in Luleå, whom she only hears from on Sundays.

**What they do themselves at the end:**

She reads her piece about the friendship aloud on the final night, before anyone else can.
