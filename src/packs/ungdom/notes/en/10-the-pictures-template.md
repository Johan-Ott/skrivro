# The pictures (template)

Rename the note to the chapter or the picture, like "Chapter 2, the dragon in the garage". In a chapter book the pictures carry part of the story. Make one for each picture, and put the Picture here label where it belongs in the text.

**Where in the text:** chapter and passage, and whether the picture is large, small or fills a whole spread.

**The moment:** exactly what is happening in the picture. Choose the most exciting, funny or emotional moment.

**What the picture shows that the text doesn't say:** a facial expression, a detail in the background, a joke only the picture has.

**What the text no longer needs to say:** descriptions the picture takes over, so you can cut them.

**The characters:** who is in it, and what they look like. Keep their appearance the same throughout the book.

**The mood:** colour, light and feeling. Safe, scary, mischievous?

**Something for the reader to spot:** a small thing to find, like a cat that appears in every picture.

**For the illustrator:** what you want them to know, and what they are free to invent.
