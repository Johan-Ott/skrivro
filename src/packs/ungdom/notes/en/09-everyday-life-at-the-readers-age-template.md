# Everyday life at the reader's age (template)

Rename the note to the place or time, like "School" or "The summer holidays". Fill it in so the book feels true to someone who is that age right now.

**The reader's age:** exactly which age band, and what the reader can already read and understand.

**An ordinary day:** from waking up to bedtime. What do they decide for themselves, and what do others decide?

**School:** the class, the teachers, break time, the lunch hall. Where is it safe, and where is it dangerous?

**Home:** the bedroom, the siblings, the food, the rules. What do people argue about?

**Free time:** sport, games, friends, time alone. Where do they meet when no adult is watching?

**The phone and the internet:** what they do there, what can go wrong, and how much of it should show in the book.

**What feels huge:** what a twelve- or fifteen-year-old might lie awake over. Rumours, grades, their body, being alone.

**What nobody says out loud:** to parents, to friends, to themselves.

**Words and phrases:** how young people that age talk, without slang that dates fast.

**What the reader shouldn't have to face:** subjects or details that are too much for this particular age band.
