# Checklist for the first draft

Go through this once the whole draft is written, before you start polishing sentences.

**The idea**

- Can the book's what if be said in one sentence?
- Does the novum have clear rules, a cost and limits, and are they never broken just because it's convenient?
- Does the book show consequences two and three steps out, not just the obvious one?
- Does the story take place at the moment the novum presses hardest?

**The world**

- Do the technology, the society and everyday life fit together? Would people really live like this with this technology?
- Is the book equally hard or soft all the way through, and do the numbers for distances, times and quantities add up?
- Is there at least one scene of scale or wonder?
- Does the reader meet the world through action rather than explanation?

**The characters**

- Does the main character have something personal to lose because of the new thing?
- Does their inner conflict mirror the book's big question?
- Does the antagonist, or the system, follow a logic that can be understood?
- Do alien beings and machines think in a way of their own, or are they humans in disguise?

**The storytelling**

- Does the first chapter open with someone in action, and only a handful of new terms?
- Are there passages where the technology is explained for longer than the reader can bear?
- Do the chapters end on a discovery, a decision or a question?

**The ending**

- Is the conflict resolved by rules the reader already understands?
- Is it a human choice that decides, and not just a machine?
- Does the ending answer the book's question, or leave one the reader wants to carry with them?
