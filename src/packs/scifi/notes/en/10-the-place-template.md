# The place (template)

Rename the note to the name of the place: a planet, a station, a ship, a city.

**First impression:** what does someone arriving for the first time see, hear and feel?

**The physics:** gravity, light, air, climate, length of day. How does it feel in the body?

**Why people are here:** colonisation, escape, work, punishment, or because they were born here?

**What keeps them alive:** water, energy, food. What happens if it runs out?

**How people live:** how they get around, what is cramped and what is expensive.

**The dangers:** what can kill you here, and how do people protect themselves?

**The beauty:** what makes the place worth seeing? This is where wonder lives.

**The link to elsewhere:** how far away is everything else, and how do you get there?

**A detail only the locals know:** something that brings the place to life.
