# The Shift

**The new thing in one sentence:**

An implant lets a person stay awake for thirty days, and then they sleep for ten.

**How it works:**

The implant stores the body's need for sleep and pays it all out in one long stretch.

**The limit:**

The sleep cannot be put off: after thirty days you fall asleep where you stand.

**The cost:**

Ten days asleep needs someone keeping watch, and the poor hire watchers they do not know.

**The numbers:**

Thirty days awake, ten days asleep, and the implant costs three months' pay.

**The consequences:**

No one planned for employers to start requiring the implant to get hired.
