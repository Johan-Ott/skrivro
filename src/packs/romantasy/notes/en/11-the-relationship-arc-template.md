# The relationship arc (template)

Rename the note to the couple's names.

**The trope:** which trope carries the relationship? Enemies to lovers, arranged marriage, only one bed. Why does this one force out their inner conflicts, and what turn of your own do you give it?

**Why not:** each lead's reasons this can never become anything. One outer and one inner reason each.

**What binds them:** the pact, the quest or the bond that means they cannot walk away from each other.

**The bond and the choice:** if magic, a bargain or fate pulls them together, in which scene do they still choose each other themselves?

**The steps:** five to seven steps from resistance to love. For example: hate, grudging respect, a life saved, a laugh, an almost-kiss, a confession, a night.

**What the reader knows:** which secrets does the reader see that the two do not, and in which chapter do they collide?

**The heat level:** closed door, half open or open? Where in the book do the intimate scenes fall, and what does each one change between them?

**The midpoint:** what happens between them, and what happens in the world at the same time?

**The betrayal and the break-up:** what drives them apart, and how is it rooted in both their wounds?

**The reconciliation:** what must whoever betrayed do to earn forgiveness, and how long may the other hesitate?

**The grand gesture:** who does what, and why can the other no longer doubt it?

**The ending:** happily ever after, or happy for now? Which image shows the reader the bond will hold?
