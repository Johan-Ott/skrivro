# The relationship arc (template)

Rename the note to the couple's names.

**The trope:** which trope carries the relationship? Enemies to lovers, forced marriage, only one bed. Why does this one force out their inner conflicts?

**Why not:** each lead's reasons this can never become anything. One outer and one inner reason each.

**What binds them:** the pact, the quest or the bond that means they cannot walk away from each other.

**The steps:** five to seven steps from resistance to love. For example: hate, grudging respect, a life saved, a laugh, an almost-kiss, a confession, a night.

**The heat level:** closed door, half open or open? Where in the book do the intimate scenes fall?

**The midpoint:** what happens between them, and what happens in the world at the same time?

**The break-up:** what drives them apart, and how is it rooted in both their wounds?

**The grand gesture:** who does what, and why can the other no longer doubt it?

**The ending:** happily ever after, or happy for now? Which image shows the reader the bond will hold?
