# Two arcs braided

This book's structure follows both arcs. Ask the same two questions of every chapter:

**What happens between them?** Resistance, being forced to work together, trust, betrayal, choice.

**What happens in the world?** The quest, the threat, the magic awakening, the power closing in on them.

The strongest chapters are those where the answers are tied together: when a turn in the world forces a turn between them, or the other way round. The midpoint is the clearest example, where the first kiss comes as the fantasy plot turns.
