# The magic system (template)

Rename the note to what the magic is called in your world.

**Who has the magic:** and who does not? How does society see those who have it?

**The source:** where does it come from? Blood, stars, oaths, a god, the land itself.

**The rules:** what can it do, and what can it not? Write two or three rules the reader must be able to trust.

**The price:** what does each use cost? Pain, memory, years, a piece of the soul.

**The limit:** what can never be done, however much you pay?

**How it feels:** what do you see, hear and feel when it is used? Give it senses.

**The forbidden:** which magic is forbidden, by whom, and why does someone want to use it anyway?

**The bond:** how does the magic tie into the love story? A bond that shares feelings, a curse that touches them both, a power only the other can wake.

**At the end:** what price is paid in the deciding battle, and has the reader seen it coming?
