# Common pitfalls

- **Insta-love.** Let them earn each other. Attraction is not the same as love.
- **A world without a price.** Magic that always works takes the tension away.
- **Misunderstandings one talk could fix.** Give the conflict a real cause: loyalties, secrets, duty.
- **A passive main character.** She or he should choose, not just be rescued.
- **The romance vanishes in the final battle.** The grand gesture should be part of the battle, not after it.
