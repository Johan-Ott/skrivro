# Checklist for the first draft

Check once the draft is done:

- Do both main characters want something concrete, and do their goals stand in each other's way?
- Does the magic have rules, a price and limits, and is it never used to solve something for free?
- Does the tension between them grow in every part, even when they are apart?
- Does the betrayal have roots early in the book, so that it feels inevitable afterwards?
- Does love decide the battle, so that the two arcs meet at the end?
- Is the ending happy, or does it promise something that holds to the next book?
