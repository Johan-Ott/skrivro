# Checklist for the first draft

Read the draft through once and check these before you start polishing sentences.

**The love story**

- Do the two meet early, within the first few chapters?
- Do both have a concrete goal, and do the goals stand in each other's way?
- Is there an outside reason that binds them, so they cannot simply walk away?
- Does the tension build in steps, and does each step take or give something?
- Is it clear in every intimate scene that both want it?
- Is the break-up rooted in their wounds and loyalties, not in a talk they should have had?
- Does the book end happily, or with a promise that holds?

**The world**

- Does the magic have rules, a price and a limit, and has the reader seen them before they decide anything?
- Does the magic never solve a problem for free at the end?
- Do the powers around them want something of their own, even off the page?
- Is the antagonist understandable, and do they strike at the relationship and not only at the realm?

**The braid**

- Do the midpoints of the love story and the world fall in the same chapter, or close together?
- Is the betrayal planted early, so it feels inevitable looking back?
- Is the grand gesture part of the final battle?
- Do both points of view carry roughly equal weight, and do they sound different?

**The reader**

- After the first chapter, does the reader know the book's tone and heat level?
- Do most chapters end on something that makes them read on?
- Has the world changed by the end, because of what the two did?
