# The main character (template)

Rename the note to the character's name.

**Wants:** what they chase when the book begins.

**Needs:** what they must learn to become whole.

**The wound:** what makes them afraid to trust anyone.

**The gift:** magic, heritage or skill, and what it costs.

**The secret:** what they do not want the love interest to know.

**What they risk:** in the world, and between them.
