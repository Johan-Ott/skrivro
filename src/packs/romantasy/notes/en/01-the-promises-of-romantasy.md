# The promises of romantasy

A romantasy reader expects two things at once, and both must be kept.

- **A love story at the heart.** The relationship drives the book as much as the adventure. Take it away and the plot should fall apart.
- **A world that matters.** The magic, the power and the dangers create the obstacles to love, not just the backdrop.
- **A slow burn.** The tension builds over many chapters: resistance, closeness, almost, and only then something more.
- **An ending that rewards.** A happy ending, or a promise that holds to the next book in the series. A sad ending breaks the genre.
- **Stakes for both.** What is at stake in the world should also be at stake between them.
