# The love interest (template)

Rename the note to the character's name.

**First impression:** why does the main character dislike or distrust them? What is true in that impression, and what is wrong?

**Want:** what are they chasing, and why does it stand in the way of the main character's goal?

**The loyalty:** who or what do they serve? A court, an oath, a family. Why does it clash with love?

**The wound and the lie:** what taught them not to trust anyone, and what false belief came with it?

**What they fear in love:** what do they think will happen if they show what they feel?

**The other side:** what shows when the wall comes down? The tenderness, the humour, what they do when no one is watching.

**The line:** what will they never do, however dark they are? Readers forgive a dangerous love interest a great deal, but only if the line shows early and holds when it counts.

**Why them:** what do they see in the main character that no one else does?

**The power:** what do they have that the main character lacks, and how do they show they will not abuse it between them?

**The secret:** what are they hiding, and how does it become the betrayal?

**The voice:** if they have their own point of view, how does it differ from the main character's? Rhythm, humour, what they notice.

**The arc:** how do they change, and what must they give up?

**The grand gesture:** what do they sacrifice at the end, and why does it mean the most to this particular main character?
