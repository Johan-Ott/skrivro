# Tropes and devices

How a trope works is in the guide Tropes and promises. In romantasy a trope is often carried by the world: the rule costs something in magic or power. Make one note per trope with the template The trope in Relationship.

- **Enemies on opposite sides (enemies to lovers).** _Works when_ loyalty has a real price. _Cliché when_ the sides are black and white and switching costs nothing.
- **The pact (fae bargain).** _Works when_ the words are exact and have consequences. _Cliché when_ nothing in it is ever used.
- **Captive of the enemy (captive).** _Works when_ the power is uneven but moves both ways. _Cliché when_ the captive is angry for three chapters and then in love.
- **The fated bond (fated mates).** _Works when_ the bond is a burden they must choose anyway. _Cliché when_ it replaces all feeling.
- **Forbidden realms (forbidden love).** _Works when_ the ban has political consequences. _Cliché when_ the rule falls in the last chapter.
- **The academy or the trial (magic academy).** _Works when_ the danger is real and the rivalry grows. _Cliché when_ the hero is the best without training.
- **The court intrigue.** _Works when_ every conversation is a move in a game. _Cliché when_ the hero alone doesn't understand the rules and wins anyway.
- **The grey antagonist.** _Works when_ their values stand against the protagonist's and nothing is excused. _Cliché when_ it all turns out to be a misunderstanding.
- **The chosen one with a price.** _Works when_ the power costs and shapes the relationship. _Cliché when_ the hero is unique for no reason in the story.
- **The morally grey love interest (morally grey).** _Works when_ the greyness has a price and is not just an aesthetic. _Cliché when_ everything grey turns out to have a good explanation.
- **Hidden nature (hidden identity).** _Works when_ the truth is planted early. _Cliché when_ it comes from nowhere.

**Make it yours.** Change one condition, not the trope: who holds the power, what the magic costs, who cracks first.
