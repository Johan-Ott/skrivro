# The oath at the ford

**The words:**

"I take you across the border, and you never lie to me while you stand on my land."

**Who bound themselves:**

He bound himself to the truth, she only to a crossing, so he bound himself most.

**The price if it is broken:**

Whoever breaks the oath loses their voice for as long as the other lives.

**The loophole:**

"My land": she owns more than he knows, all the way up to the king's city.

**What it forces them into:**

He must answer truly whatever she asks, so she stops asking about what matters.

**What it hides:**

Only she knows that whoever brings someone across the ford answers for his crimes too.

**When it is tested:**

In chapter 22 she asks who he smuggles for, and he keeps the oath though the answer condemns them both.
